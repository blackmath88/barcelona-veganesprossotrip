01
THE MAIN RECOMMENDATION

GRÀCIA

This was the Barcelona we loved most. Small streets, little squares, cafés, bars, independent shops and a neighbourhood rhythm that feels like actual city life rather than a sightseeing corridor.

DO THIS

Do not build a route. Pick a point around Vila de Gràcia, Carrer de Verdi or Plaça del Sol and then just drift. Sit down when somewhere looks good. Turn into the smaller street.

WHY

It feels compact, human-scale and local. The pleasure is not a landmark — it is spending a few hours there without needing one.

02
YOUR WORK BASE / CIUTAT VELLA

TRAFALGAR 6

Your office is extremely central: Despacho 28, Carrer de Trafalgar 6. Useful as a starting point — but we would not let “central” dictate where you spend all your free time.

OUR MOVE

Finish work, then head out of the old-centre gravity field. Gràcia is the obvious evening target; Encants and Poblenou work well for a different side of the city.

03
THE EXCEPTION TO OUR GAUDÍ FATIGUE

SAGRADA
FAMÍLIA

We did not really fall for the whole Gaudí trail. But Sagrada Família is different: huge, strange and genuinely worth seeing from the inside.

IMPORTANT

Book the entrance early. Time slots can sell out, so do not assume you can simply decide to go that afternoon.

OUR TAKE

If you want one big-ticket Barcelona monument, make it this one — and then go back to neighbourhood life.

04
A DIFFERENT BARCELONA

ELS
ENCANTS

Worth it less as “the best flea market ever” and more because it pulls you into a completely different Barcelona: Glòries, infrastructure, old industry, everyday trading — not old town postcard scenery.

DO THIS

Browse without a mission. Look up at the mirrored roof, take the ramps, then keep moving east instead of returning to the centre.

05
OLD INDUSTRY → SEA

POBLENOU

A nice counterpoint to Gràcia. Less immediately charming, more mixed: former industrial streets, studios, new development, neighbourhood cafés — and eventually the sea.

BEST FLOW

Encants → Glòries → wander through Poblenou → Rambla del Poblenou → Bogatell. It works because the city keeps changing around you.

END HERE

If the weather works, finish at Bogatell rather than turning the day back into another old-town loop.
