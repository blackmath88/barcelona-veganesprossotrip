LIKE A LIST.

We liked the city most when it felt like a city: little squares, independent streets, coffee, food, markets, browsing and walking. Gràcia was the clear favourite. Els Encants and Poblenou were useful because they showed a totally different Barcelona.

01 / GRÀCIA

Make real time for it. Wander around Verdi, Virreina, Plaça del Sol and Vila de Gràcia.

02 / SAGRADA

Yes, even if you are not doing a Gaudí pilgrimage. Book entrance early; the slots sell out.

03 / LA RAMBLA

We would not plan time around it. Cross it if necessary, but do not make it the trip.

04 / TRAFALGAR 6

02
THE WORK BASE

TRAFALGAR 6

The office is at Carrer de Trafalgar 6, just above the old city and close to Arc de Triomf. That makes Poblenou a very natural after-work direction rather than another loop through the tourist centre.

EASTWARD

Walk or ride toward Arc de Triomf, Glòries and Poblenou. It gradually changes from monumental centre to everyday neighbourhood and old industrial fabric.

03
THE BIG SIGHT

SAGRADA
FAMÍLIA

We were not particularly interested in doing a whole Gaudí circuit. Sagrada Família was the exception: if you want to see it properly, go inside.

BOOK EARLY

Entrance slots sell out. Do not rely on buying tickets when you get there.

04
ANOTHER SIDE OF THE CITY

ENCANTS →
POBLENOU

Els Encants was not “the best market ever”, but it was worth seeing because it moved us out of old-town Barcelona. The mirrored roof is great, the market is huge, and from there the city naturally unfolds east toward Poblenou.

OUR TAKE

Good, not great — sometimes a little touristy — but there are treasures and the architecture of the roof is worth seeing.

KEEP GOING

Do not turn back toward the centre. Continue through Glòries into Poblenou and, if the weather is right, finish near Bogatell.

05
THE EASTWARD DRIFT

POBLENOU

A nice urban contrast from the office and Encants: everyday streets, old industrial fabric, a beach ending and a slower eastward line.

OUR BIAS

NO LA RAMBLA DAY.

We would spend that time in Gràcia, walking east from the office, or seeing Sagrada Família properly. Barcelona got better for us the moment we stopped treating the tourist centre as the default.
